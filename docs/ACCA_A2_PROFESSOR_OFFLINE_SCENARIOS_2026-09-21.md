# ACCA A2 and offline Professor scenarios — 21 September 2026

ACCA FR A2 extends the explicit local curriculum Preview with a second independently lazy-loaded model lesson. It covers relevance, faithful representation, enhancing characteristics, prudence/neutrality and the cost constraint through original explanations, a comparison map, an applied case, practice and a local checkpoint.

The Professor scenario pack contains ten authored offline expectations: five for A1 and five for the English storytelling Golden Lesson. They exercise correct reasoning, help, misconception, self-correction, supported alternatives, hesitation, answer disclosure and missing acoustic evidence. They are acceptance expectations, not model executions or learner results.

A2 remains available only with `?curriculumPreview=1`. Its Professor and podcast assets are outlines only. No Supabase change, publication, provider call, audio generation, worker change, deployment workflow or saved progress is included.
