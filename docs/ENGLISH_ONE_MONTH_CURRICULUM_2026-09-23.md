# English one-month curriculum — 2026-09-23

## Current outcome

The local English course now contains four five-day study weeks:

- **20 complete written lessons**;
- **10 Everyday English lessons**;
- **10 Technical English lessons**;
- approximately **45–55 minutes per lesson**;
- one authored lesson per study day;
- an exact **50/50 curriculum split**.

This checkpoint supersedes the ten-lesson checkpoint as the current local English scope. It does not publish or deploy the application.

## Permanent direction

Everyday English is broad general English. Dublin can appear as an occasional setting, but it is not the centre of the curriculum.

The technical half prioritises:

1. finance;
2. accounting;
3. tax;
4. legal and compliance;
5. payroll;
6. treasury.

Technical cases use supplied fictional facts and amounts. The learner must distinguish fact, assumption, judgement, workflow status and final conclusion rather than memorise unsupported rules.

## New Everyday English depth

### E6 — Shopping and returns

Compare products by useful criteria, describe a problem using evidence, ask which remedy is available and confirm the real next step without inventing store policy.

### E7 — Restaurants

Express preferences, order in manageable parts, ask precise ingredient questions and resolve an order or bill mismatch without unnecessary blame.

### E8 — Family plans

Arrange playdates and weekends, clarify responsibility and timing, communicate changes early and close with an accurate read-back.

### E9 — News and politics

Separate source, fact, interpretation and opinion; express a bounded view; disagree with the argument rather than the person; repair or close a tense exchange.

### E10 — TV, films and hobbies

Describe premise and tone without spoilers, recommend for another person’s taste, react with precision and turn a hobby answer into a natural conversation.

## New Technical English depth

### P6 — Month-end and accounting

Explain control totals, signed reconciling items, residual differences, errors, estimates, proposed journals, approvals and sign-off.

### P7 — Tax

Fix jurisdiction, entity, tax type, period and transaction; separate fact, assumption, filing position and legal conclusion; protect payment and filing deadlines.

### P8 — Payroll

Validate inputs, reconcile supplied gross-to-net data, keep employer cost separate, answer employee queries with privacy and preserve correction status and audit trail.

### P9 — Treasury

Distinguish bank balance, restricted cash, available cash and forecast closing; communicate liquidity and FX exposure; preserve payment validation, approval and release controls.

### P10 — Contracts and compliance

Parse actor, obligation, condition and timing; separate clause, facts and legal interpretation; describe possible non-compliance without declaring an unsupported breach.

## Lesson contract

Each new lesson contains:

- four guided teaching sections with Portuguese support;
- six technical or everyday terms in context;
- one visual reasoning table;
- one fictional case study;
- five checkpoint questions;
- three practice exercises;
- reviewed public sources;
- explicit professional and safety boundaries.

## Technical and operational boundaries

The expansion:

- does not publish, deploy or modify Supabase;
- does not call a paid AI, voice or audio provider;
- does not contact a school, employer, shop, restaurant, bank, tax authority or legal adviser;
- does not calculate live tax or payroll liabilities;
- does not provide accounting, tax, legal, employment, investment or treasury advice;
- does not infer listening, pronunciation, fluency or a CEFR certification from written work.
