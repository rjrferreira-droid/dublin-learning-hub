# ACCA FR B7–B8 local curriculum — 21 September 2026

B7 and B8 extend the explicit `?curriculumPreview=1` curriculum with two independently lazy-loaded written lessons. B7 covers IAS 37 provision recognition and measurement, contingencies and IAS 10 adjusting/non-adjusting events. B8 covers IAS 12 current tax, tax bases, temporary differences, deferred tax recognition and the closing-balance movement.

Each lesson contains four teaching sections, six English/Portuguese terms, an original visual calculation, a worked case, three supported exercises, five local checkpoint questions, a Premium Audio outline and an offline Professor guide. ACCA’s September 2026–June 2027 syllabus landing page and the IFRS Foundation’s IAS 37, IAS 10 and IAS 12 pages are the authoritative sources. Exact selected-sitting outcome wording remains a publication gate.

The lessons are authored local previews, not ACCA-approved tuition, jurisdiction-specific tax advice, complete syllabus coverage, persisted assessment or evidence of exam readiness. Professor and podcast assets remain inactive outlines.
