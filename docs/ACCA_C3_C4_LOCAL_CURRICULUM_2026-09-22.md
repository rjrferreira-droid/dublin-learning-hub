# ACCA FR C3–C4 local curriculum — 22 September 2026

C3 and C4 extend the explicit `?curriculumPreview=1` curriculum with two independently lazy-loaded written lessons. C3 addresses the limitations of ratio analysis, comparability effects of policies and estimates, the complementary usefulness of cash-flow and accrual information, and profit-versus-EPS trends. C4 changes the interpretation lens for not-for-profit and public sector entities, covering mission, accountability, funding restrictions, budgets, financial sustainability, service potential, economy, efficiency, effectiveness, outputs and outcomes.

Each lesson contains four teaching sections, six English/Portuguese terms, an original visual analysis, a worked case, three supported exercises, five local checkpoint questions, a Premium Audio outline and an offline Professor guide. Ten additional offline Professor scenarios cover correct reasoning, misconceptions, help requests, self-correction and requested worked support. ACCA’s September 2026–June 2027 syllabus and interpretation resources, IAS 33, and the IPSASB Conceptual Framework and 2026 Handbook are the authoritative sources. Exact selected-sitting outcome wording and the relevant entity or jurisdictional reporting framework remain publication gates.

The lessons are authored local previews, not ACCA-approved tuition, investment or public-policy advice, complete syllabus coverage, persisted assessment or evidence of exam readiness. Professor and podcast assets remain inactive outlines. No provider call, audio generation, database change, Supabase operation, deployment, publication, learner-progress write or paid service is included.
