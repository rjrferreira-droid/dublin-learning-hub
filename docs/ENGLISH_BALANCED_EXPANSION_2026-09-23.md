# English balanced expansion — 2026-09-23

## Outcome

The local English course now contains ten reviewed written lessons with an exact 5/5 split:

- five everyday-English lessons;
- five professional-English lessons.

This checkpoint adds:

- **E5 — School conversations:** share attributed observations, ask neutral questions, distinguish advice from obligation and confirm a follow-up without inventing an agreement;
- **P5 — Job interviews:** give concise evidence-based answers, distinguish individual contribution from team outcome and connect transferable experience to a real role requirement.

## Learning design

Both lessons follow the existing written contract:

- four guided sections with Portuguese support;
- six terms in context;
- one visual reasoning table;
- one fictional case study;
- five checkpoint questions;
- three practice exercises;
- reviewed public sources.

All examples, scenarios and exercises are original. The sources support language and interaction choices; their exercises are not copied.

## Boundaries

This is a local content expansion only. It:

- does not publish or deploy the application;
- does not write to Supabase or another database;
- does not call a voice or paid AI provider;
- does not contact a school or employer;
- does not infer pronunciation, live listening ability or a CEFR certification from written work;
- does not provide medical, safeguarding, immigration, employment-law or school-policy advice.

## Verification target

The local English test suite must confirm:

- ten ordered local lessons;
- five everyday and five professional lessons;
- exact fail-closed identity matching for E5 and P5;
- complete written contracts for the new modules;
- resolution through the reviewed lazy loader;
- no network, database, voice or paid-provider execution path in the local English sources.
