## Authenticated Audio preparation

The unmounted `written-audio-preview.ts` candidate uses that same authenticated resolver. Finance and Payroll overviews use the existing Portuguese support paragraphs; English uses the first teaching paragraph of each section. This selection is explicitly an overview, not a full narration. All sections retain complete source paragraphs. Scripts over 4,000 characters fail rather than silently truncate. Worked-case answers and browser scripts/answers are excluded. English 8 explicitly preserves its unverified Irish-audio/listening boundary.

Script SHA256 identifies the exact text; a separate source fingerprint binds lesson/track/sequence/content version, language and overview policy. This is not approval of a rendered audio cache: future cache reuse must also match the reviewed provider/model/voice/format configuration and durable receipt. No provider is selected or invoked and no cache is signed by this preparation function.

The local platform fixture passes all 24 prepared audio identities into the existing shared attempt orchestrator against the real zero-budget database. Every attempt must be denied by the global budget before prepare, submission, generation or storage callbacks. This verifies real HTTP/RPC wiring and the denial path; it does not activate future-lesson Audio or claim successful TTS quality.
