# ACCA FR C1–C2 local curriculum — 22 September 2026

C1 and C2 extend the explicit `?curriculumPreview=1` curriculum with two independently lazy-loaded written lessons. C1 covers limits of historical information, presentation incentives and window dressing, seasonality and reporting-date balances, late acquisitions, and limitations of consolidated analysis. C2 covers profitability and efficiency, liquidity and working-capital days, gearing and investor measures, cash-flow corroboration, historical-cost versus current-value effects, non-financial information and stakeholder-specific conclusions.

Each lesson contains four teaching sections, six English/Portuguese terms, an original visual analysis, a worked case, three supported exercises, five local checkpoint questions, a Premium Audio outline and an offline Professor guide. Ten additional offline Professor scenarios cover correct reasoning, misconceptions, help requests, self-correction and requested worked support. C2 states its formula conventions and reconciles operating margin multiplied by asset turnover to ROCE. ACCA’s September 2026–June 2027 syllabus and interpretation resources, together with the IFRS Foundation’s Conceptual Framework and IFRS 10 pages, are the authoritative sources. Exact selected-sitting outcome wording remains a publication gate.

The lessons are authored local previews, not ACCA-approved tuition, audit or investment advice, complete syllabus coverage, persisted assessment or evidence of exam readiness. Professor and podcast assets remain inactive outlines. No provider call, audio generation, database change, Supabase operation, deployment, publication, learner-progress write or paid service is included.
